# YUV.AI Cover System v2 — "Editorial Poster" (reverse-engineered style DNA)

Source DNA: SHELLY sports-editorial posters (basketball/tennis/football) + cinematic Joker
teaching covers. 12 Midjourney describes analyzed. This spec is the law for every cover.

## The 7 signature ingredients (what makes the style)
1. **Giant condensed display type, slightly italic** — fills 60–80% of frame width, 1–2 stacked
   lines. Flat fill, no gradient on letters. EN: Anton + skewX(-8°). HE: Rubik 900 + skewX(-6°).
2. **Subject INTERLEAVED with the type** — headline sits BEHIND the body but IN FRONT of the
   background; a limb/prop overlaps letters. Built from photo + its cutout (photo → text → cutout).
3. **Ticker strips** — small bold caps repeated with ✦ separators ("ALL DAY ✦ EVERY PLAY ✦ NO
   LIMITS") at the top edge and/or floating above the headline. Letter-spaced, white or accent.
4. **Keyword stack** — 3–4 power words, right-aligned column, small condensed caps + 2–3 thin
   line icons (star / globe / target) beneath. Bottom-right corner.
5. **Hero camera angle** — ultra-low angle full body (sports) or symmetric close-up / high-angle
   (cinema). Subject larger-than-life, action frozen mid-move.
6. **Depth-of-field props** — one foreground element blurred/huge (ball, shoe, dirt spray) that
   *crosses* the type. This is the dopamine trick — never skip it when the photo allows.
7. **One loud accent against one saturated field** — neon yellow `#E9FF3D` on vivid blue sky
   (HYPE) or pure white type on dark cinematic duotone (CINEMA). Never two accents at once.

## Yuval's two series (uniform grid, two moods)
| Series | Use for | Ground | Type color | Light |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **HYPE** | tools, launches, hot takes, news | vivid gradient sky / bright location | neon yellow `#E9FF3D` | sunny, saturated, rim-lit |
| **CINEMA** | tutorials, concepts, "film school"-style series | dark photo, **pink/cyan split-light duotone** (the Neon-Phoenix version of Joker's red/green) | white `#FFFFFF` | dramatic, moody |

## Type rules
- Headline ≤2 words per line, ≤2 lines. Auto-size to fill width minus 40px margins; tracking -1%.
- Line 1 higher (over chest/head), line 2 lower (crossed by legs/prop). One word may flip color.
- Ticker: 11–13px-equivalent at 1080w → 26–30px, weight 800, letter-spacing 4px, ✦ separators.
- Stack: 34–40px condensed caps, right-aligned, accent color, 4 words max.

## Photo rules
- Subject sharp, background graphic and simple (sky, court, fog — not a busy room).
- Cutout gets 1–2px rim light feel via drop-shadow in scene color; never a hard white sticker edge.
- CINEMA duotone: desaturate slightly, pink glow from one side, cyan from the other, crushed blacks.

## Anti-rules (instant style-breakers)
- No drop-shadowed rounded "YouTube sticker" text. No more than 2 type colors. No busy screenshot
  backgrounds behind the giant type. No centered-everything — the grid energy comes from diagonal
  tension between type blocks and the figure. Subject must never be fully behind ALL text (face stays clear).
