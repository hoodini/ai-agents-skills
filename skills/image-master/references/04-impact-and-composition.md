# 04 — Visual Impact: Color, Composition, Drama, Narrative

Realism gets you past inspection; **impact** wins. Each lever pairs the evidence (why it works on the brain/eye) with a copy-paste prompt fragment.

## 1. COLOR RECIPES (arousal + separation)
- **Teal–orange complementary split.** Highest exposure-value contrast of any complementary pair; the eye is most sensitive to orange skin/fur against teal, so the subject pops and warm/cool splits depth (warm advances, cool recedes). → `warm orange key light on the subject, deep teal-cyan shadows and background, complementary color separation`
- **Single saturated accent on a desaturated field.** Saturated color spikes arousal and pupil dilation; muted surroundings make it an irresistible focal magnet. → `near-monochrome muted palette with one vivid saturated [red] accent, color isolation`
- **Red for urgency/adrenaline.** Tied to danger; fastest color to grab attention (ERP-confirmed). Use sparingly — prolonged red reads as stress. → `a single charge of crimson red signaling danger`
- **"Dopamine" high-key pop** (joy/awe): `electric blues, hot pinks, sunny yellows, vibrant high-saturation palette`

## 2. COMPOSITION (drive the eye)
- **Leading lines terminating ON the subject.** Eye-tracking (Yang 2025): lines + a prominent subject scored highest on aesthetics and produced *longer dwell, fewer scattered fixations* — concentrated engagement. The rule: put a clear subject at the end of the lines. → `strong leading lines (road / river / fence / shaft of light) converging on the subject at the focal point`
- **Thirds vs golden ratio vs central dominance.** Thirds = natural tension/balance at a grid intersection; golden spiral pulls focus tighter; for a single hero *punch*, central dominance + symmetry can hit hardest. → `subject on the right-third power point, horizon on the lower third` OR `dead-center symmetrical composition, single dominant subject`
- **Negative space** = clarity + the sublime; minimalism amplifies awe. → `vast negative space, minimalist composition, tiny subject isolated in an empty frame`
- **Figure–ground separation** makes the subject instantly readable. → `bright rim-lit subject against a dark background, strong figure-ground separation`

## 3. TENSION / DRAMA DEVICES
- **Chiaroscuro / low-key / Rembrandt.** Hard light + deep shadow builds 3D depth on a 2D plane; signals mystery, dread, gravitas. → `dramatic chiaroscuro, single hard key light, deep falloff into black, film-noir mood`
- **Scale juxtaposition → awe.** A tiny subject against overwhelming vastness magnifies grandeur (the "small self," dopamine/serotonin, the sublime). → `a lone tiny figure dwarfed by a colossal [cliff / storm / sky], overwhelming sense of scale`
- **The decisive moment.** A single frozen peak-action instant (the strike, the catch, the confrontation) — what World Press / Wildlife Photographer winners reward. → `the decisive moment frozen at peak action, split-second tension`
- **Implied motion / the gaze.** Gaze direction acts as a leading line; eye contact with the viewer is a confrontational hook. → `the subject's intense gaze locked on the viewer` OR `looking off-frame toward unseen tension, directional gaze leading the eye`
- **Rule of odds.** Odd-numbered groups (esp. three) read more dynamic than even/balanced. → `three subjects arranged asymmetrically`

## 4. SINGLE-IMAGE NARRATIVE (the judges' "distinct visual narrative")
- **Juxtaposition of opposites** — the most iconic photojournalism pairs contradictory elements in one frame (life/decay, fragile/monstrous, beauty/threat). 2025 Wildlife grand-title: a wild hyena inside an abandoned human town — nature reclaiming civilization. → `a single frame juxtaposing [the wild against the man-made / the tender against the violent], one image telling a whole story through contradiction`
- **Anthropomorphic emotional hook** — a face/gesture reading as human emotion is the universal, language-transcending viral hook (emotional contagion). → `the animal's expression reading as unmistakable [grief / fear / defiance / tenderness]`
- **Photojournalistic "truth" texture** — judges value images that read as *witnessed*, not rendered. → `documentary photojournalism, available light, candid unposed authenticity, true-to-life grain`

## 5. The "winning-frame" skeleton
> `[CAMERA: angle + lens character + DoF]. [ENVIRONMENT with explicit horizon + spatial anchors]. [SINGLE HERO SUBJECT + precise pose/gaze]. [LIGHTING: one key + color-temperature contrast]. [COLOR recipe]. [NARRATIVE DEVICE]. [TEXTURE callouts for 4K]. [exact text, placement, material if any].`

Lock it as a composition, render at native resolution, then **edit the one weak element** (hand/face/text) rather than re-rolling — playing to the model's strengths.

## 6. Contest rubric → lever map
| Judging criterion | Levers to pull |
|---|---|
| Distinct visual narrative / striking POV | unusual camera (worm's-eye, top-down, Dutch); juxtaposition of opposites; the gaze |
| Technical craft (anatomy/texture/text) | ch.01 anatomy + ch.02 materials/light + ch.03 text + native 4K |
| Range | span disciplines across the set (wildlife/portrait/architecture/product/concept/graphic/abstract) |
| Impact & innovation | teal-orange or single-accent color; chiaroscuro; scale/awe; the decisive moment |
